# Where I stand on AI
<a href="https://youtu.be/DDf25rNyT1A">Experts<a/> diagnosed <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fascism_in_the_United_States#Donald_Trump_and_fascism">Trump<a/> (and thus the system behind him) to be a <a href="https://maisonfrancaise.columbia.edu/sites/maisonfrancaise.columbia.edu/files/content/Is%20It%20Fascism_%20A%20Leading%20Historian%20Changes%20His%20Mind.%20-%20The%20New%20York%20Times.pdf">fascist<a/>. To get stronger, fascism usually <a href="https://youtu.be/DDf25rNyT1A?t=509">builds an alliance<a/> with <a href="https://youtu.be/Grpw9BFx-E8?t=1">the capital<a/>. <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/05/trump-power-ai-techscape">Leaders<a/> of the <a href="https://stersoftware.com/news/how-major-ai-companies-are-becoming-entangled-with-the-trump-administration-2026-07-03-avond/">main AI companies<a/> showed themselves many times to be part of the <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/08/billionaires-tech-oligarchs">US oligarchy<a/>. Some experts talk of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technofeudalism">techno-feudalism<a/>, others go further and name the situation as a capitalism of total <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsumption_(Marxism)">subsumption<a/> and of <a href="https://youtu.be/i7I2jGy7AZc?t=1555">total control<a/>.

In democracies, universities have the role to develop and spread knowledge to fight power when it lies to the public. Likewise, I believe mathematicians and scientists have the moral obligation to work with the aim to benefit human kind, or at least not to worsen the human situation (this remark could be enlarged to include the other inhabitants of our world). This is not the case when one promotes companies who are co-creating fascist regimes, who are known for <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/may/05/publishers-sue-meta-copyright-ai">stealing<a/> <a href="https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-07-23/judge-approves-anthropics-1-5-billion-settlement-with-authors">intellectual<a/> <a href="https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/08/u-s-textbook-authors-sue-openai-for-copyright-infringement/">property<a/>, <a href="https://www.cbc.ca/player/play/video/9.7311779">methodically<a/> destroying antique books and making their content private (do you hear the sound of obscurantism?), <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cvqi8UG5YlA">undermining our chances<a/> to limit<a href="https://unu.edu/inweh/collection/environmental-cost-of-AIs-Enrgy-Use-Carbon-water-and-land-footprints"> the consequences of global warming<a/>, encouraging excessive consumption of resources (which is the main reason for the extinction of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropocene#Nature_of_human_effects">biodiversity in number of species and number of individuals<a/>), developing weapons for <a href="https://mondediplo.com/2025/11/02tech">military purposes and mass surveillance<a/>, destroying our spaces of creation and freedom. 

The good news is the following: AI companies base their economy on a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_bubble#Speculation">financial<a/> <a href="https://youtu.be/Yu-wqYCyInU?t=2669">bubble<a/>, very close to a <a href="https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-07-29/nvidias-750-billion-ai-bet-deepens-fears-of-circular-tech-bubble">circular tech bubble<a/>. It means that these companies need to keep our trust at all cost. So not only do we have a responsibility not to support companies who make fascism possible, but we also have the responsibility not to help them in making us public billboards for their businesses. Their survival partially depends on our current reaction. Mathematicians, academics in general, are considered as part of the elites of their countries. A signal from us can make a difference in the behavior of the population. The AI companies perfectly understood that, and that is why they try to convert scientists in their favour so badly. Don't fall in the trap, my friend. For the ones who don't have the power and the privileges we have, we need to fight.

The <a href="https://atecopol.hypotheses.org/13082">objection of conscience<a/> raised by Atécopol is the closest stance to mine. I naturally agree with the analysis of <a href="https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/lehrstuhl/lazic/WhereIStand/Principles.pdf">Vladimir Lazić<a/> and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02859">Max Weinreich<a/>.
